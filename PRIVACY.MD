What the app stores

Twix4U Tool has no accounts and no servers of its own. The settings you choose (for example which module is on, your crosshair style, or your last game) are stored only on your phone. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Permissions and why
Display over other apps and foreground service: to show the crosshair and the ping box on top of your game.
Internet, network state and Wi-Fi state: for the Ping Monitor and to load ads.
Sync settings: for the Auto-Sync module, which turns background account syncing off and on.
Shizuku access: to apply system tweaks on your phone. These stay on your device.
Network use
The Ping Monitor connects to a public server (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 or Google 8.8.8.8) only to measure speed.
If you choose a Private DNS in the Ping Monitor, your phone's DNS setting is changed to Cloudflare or Google. You can change it back any time.
Ads

Premium modules are unlocked by watching a rewarded ad provided by Unity Ads. Unity may collect device information such as your advertising ID, IP address and device details to show and measure ads. See Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

Twix4U Tool is not directed to children under 13.
